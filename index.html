how i lead

who / where

i lead from the brief, not the backlog. i'm usually the one saying "that's not the actual problem" in the kickoff — then i prototype the answer, bring engineering in early, and ship in slices.

  • own the outcome, not the file. dozens of interviews with salespeople and revops shaped sumble's upload flow and admin dashboard — then i saw them through to production.
  • build the foundation. two companies, two design systems from scratch. at sumble, figma components map 1:1 to production react.
  • set the bar. i define the motion and interaction standards engineers build to — by shipping working prototypes instead of specs.
  • ship it myself. i prototype with claude and either hand it off or push it to production — sumble's sidebar is my code.
  • work across the company. 2–10 person teams with product, engineering, data, customer success and founders — plus marketing, including sumble's homepage launch.
